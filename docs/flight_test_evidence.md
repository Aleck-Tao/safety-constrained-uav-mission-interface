# Physical UAV Evidence Boundary

The two still images in `assets/` were extracted from original outdoor UAV field-test videos recorded on 1 July 2026. They verify that a physical platform was assembled and flown outdoors.

The canonical MP4 files, media metadata, SHA-256 hashes, and reproducible image-quality audit are published in [`uav-flight-video-quality-audit`](https://github.com/Aleck-Tao/uav-flight-video-quality-audit).

The footage does not establish autonomous navigation, LiDAR/stereo fusion accuracy, or VLA capability. Those claims require synchronized telemetry, calibration records, and an independent reference system.
